Federal Funding in Hawaiʻi

Where do federal dollars flow to in Hawaiʻi and what do they support?

Understanding the federal funding picture reveals Hawaiʻi's ties to the federal government and local impacts of shifts in federal priorities.

Overview

Hawaiʻi receives billions of dollars from the federal government each year, but only a fraction flows through the state budget. This tool shows all federal funding commitments in Hawaiʻi, including:

  • Direct benefits: Payments to individuals (e.g. Social Security, SNAP)
  • Pass-through funding: Federal grants to state agencies (e.g. Medicaid)
  • Contracts & grants: Federal awards to Hawaiʻi-based entities (e.g. small businesses and nonprofits)

All underlying data is available for download, and you can learn more about our sources and methodology.
