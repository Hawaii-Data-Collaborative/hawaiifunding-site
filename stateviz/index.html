Hawaiʻi State Budget Explorer

What are we investing in, and does that reflect the Hawai'i we want?

Understanding the state budget reveals Hawaiʻi's priorities and enables decisions that reflect shared values.

Overview

Our state government allocates billions of dollars each year to fund essential services across Hawaiʻi. This visualization tool makes state budget data transparent and accessible so stakeholders can:

  • Understand where dollars currently flow
  • Identify potential funding gaps
  • Assess impacts of budgetary shifts

All underlying data is available for download, and you can learn more about our sources and methodology.
